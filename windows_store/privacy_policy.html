Hearth Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Hearth is designed to keep your psychological data where it belongs: on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No cloud sync or backup.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting.
  • No cookies, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints.

Data stored locally

All tasks, mood entries, sleep logs, journal entries, practice sessions, settings, and your crisis plan are stored in a single SQLite database in your operating system's app-data directory. This database never leaves your machine unless you manually copy it.

Network access

Hearth does not make network requests. External links (for example, a 988 crisis line) open in your default web browser, not inside the app.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not have access to your data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@hearthproject.io.
