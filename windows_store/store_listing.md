## App name

Hearth

## Short description (max 500 characters)

A desktop that adapts to your psychology. Hearth helps people managing ADHD, anxiety, depression, OCD, PTSD, or bipolar organize tasks, track mood and sleep, and reach grounding practices — all offline, with no account or cloud.

## Long description

Hearth is a mental-health-aware desktop companion. Instead of treating you like a productivity machine, it reads the conditions you carry — your mood, sleep, and energy — and reshapes the day around them.

- **Today** — a daily briefing with an energy-budget meter, crisis banner, and one gentle next step.
- **Tasks** — priority + area + energy, with automatic spoon-cost estimates and energy-aware ordering.
- **Reflect** — quick mood / energy / anxiety check-ins, sleep logs, and journaling.
- **Practices** — guided breathing, grounding, meditation, and focus blocks with pre/post SUDS.
- **Rhythm** — mood, energy, and sleep trends over 7 / 14 / 30 days.
- **Crisis plan** — warning signs, coping strategies, trusted contacts, and a note to your future self.

Everything stays in a single local database on your machine. No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry.

Hearth is a personal wellness tool, not a medical device. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.

## Features

- Offline-first SQLite storage
- Mood, energy, anxiety, and sleep tracking
- Spoon-theory task planning
- Guided breathing and grounding practices
- Crisis plan and conservative crisis signals
