# Mindful Organizer - Windows Store Listing

## Short Description (100 characters max)

Organize your life mindfully. Mental health-aware productivity and task management for your desktop.

## Full Description

**Mindful Organizer** is a desktop productivity application that puts your mental well-being first. Unlike traditional task managers that pile on pressure with aggressive deadlines and overloaded dashboards, Mindful Organizer helps you stay productive while respecting your emotional state and energy levels.

Built for people who want to get things done without burning out, Mindful Organizer combines intelligent file organization, task management, DBT diary card tracking, and wellness monitoring in a single, calming interface. Your data never leaves your device -- everything is stored locally with encryption for sensitive content.

Whether you are managing daily tasks, organizing files across projects, tracking your mood with structured diary cards, or understanding how sleep affects your productivity, Mindful Organizer adapts to you rather than the other way around.

### Why Mindful Organizer?

- **Privacy-first architecture**: Zero data collection. No telemetry. No cloud dependency. Your data stays on your device.
- **Mental health awareness**: Built-in DBT diary cards, wellness tracking, mood logging, and adaptive task suggestions based on your current state.
- **Condition-aware design**: 8 themes with unique layout density, animation speed, and chrome visibility. File organizer adapts to ADHD, OCD, Depression, and Anxiety.
- **Intelligent organization**: Smart file management with condition-aware folder structures, duplicate detection, and batch rename.
- **Distraction-free design**: A calming, minimal interface with soft colors and thoughtful interactions that reduce cognitive load.

## Feature Bullets

1. **Smart Task Management** -- Create, organize, and prioritize tasks with intelligent scheduling that adapts to your energy levels and mood.
2. **DBT Diary Card** -- Structured daily tracking of emotions, urges, skills, target behaviors, and medication adherence. Condition-aware content.
3. **Wellness Tracking** -- Log mood alongside tasks to understand the connection between well-being and productivity. Automatic energy prediction from sleep and mood.
4. **Condition-Aware File Organization** -- File organizer adapts to your mental health profile: ADHD (action-based emoji folders), OCD (numbered structure), Depression (energy-tiered), Anxiety (detailed hierarchy).
5. **Guided Meditations** -- Built-in guided meditation sessions to help you reset and refocus during your workday.
6. **Encrypted Local Storage** -- All sensitive data, especially mental health entries, is encrypted at rest using industry-standard cryptography.
7. **Data Export and Import** -- Full control over your data with export to JSON and CSV formats, plus seamless backup and restore.
8. **Shareable Web Reports** -- Generate beautiful self-contained HTML reports with charts. Open in any browser, share via cloud storage, or paste into Notion.
9. **Focus Sessions** -- Built-in Pomodoro-style focus timers with mindfulness breaks that encourage sustainable work habits.
10. **Adaptive Interface** -- 8 condition-aware themes with unique layout density, animation speed, and accessibility modes including color-blind support and dyslexia-friendly font.
