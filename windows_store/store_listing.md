# Mindful Organizer - Windows Store Listing

## Short Description (100 characters max)

Organize your life mindfully. Mental health-aware productivity and task management for your desktop.

## Full Description

**Mindful Organizer** is a desktop productivity application that puts your mental well-being first. Unlike traditional task managers that pile on pressure with aggressive deadlines and overloaded dashboards, Mindful Organizer helps you stay productive while respecting your emotional state and energy levels.

Built for people who want to get things done without burning out, Mindful Organizer combines intelligent file organization, task management, and wellness tracking in a single, calming interface. Your data never leaves your device -- everything is stored locally with encryption for sensitive content.

Whether you are managing daily tasks, organizing files across projects, or tracking your mood alongside your workload, Mindful Organizer adapts to you rather than the other way around.

### Why Mindful Organizer?

- **Privacy-first architecture**: Zero data collection. No telemetry. No cloud dependency. Your data stays on your device.
- **Mental health awareness**: Built-in wellness tracking, mood logging, and adaptive task suggestions based on your current state.
- **Intelligent organization**: Machine learning-powered document clustering and smart file management that learns your patterns.
- **Distraction-free design**: A calming, minimal interface with soft colors and thoughtful interactions that reduce cognitive load.

## Feature Bullets

1. **Smart Task Management** -- Create, organize, and prioritize tasks with intelligent scheduling that adapts to your energy levels and mood.
2. **Wellness Tracking** -- Log your mood, energy, and stress levels alongside your tasks to understand the connection between well-being and productivity.
3. **Intelligent File Organization** -- AI-powered document clustering automatically groups and organizes your files into meaningful categories.
4. **Guided Meditations** -- Built-in guided meditation sessions to help you reset and refocus during your workday.
5. **Encrypted Local Storage** -- All sensitive data, especially mental health entries, is encrypted at rest using industry-standard cryptography.
6. **Custom Project Files** -- Save and share your organizational setups using portable `.mindful` project files.
7. **Data Export and Import** -- Full control over your data with export to JSON and CSV formats, plus seamless backup and restore.
8. **Adaptive Interface** -- The UI adjusts its suggestions and pacing based on your reported wellness state, reducing pressure when you need it.
9. **Focus Sessions** -- Built-in Pomodoro-style focus timers with mindfulness breaks that encourage sustainable work habits.
10. **Visualization Dashboard** -- Beautiful charts and graphs showing your productivity trends alongside wellness patterns over time.
