# Evidence-Based Mental Health Design - Research Overview

This document summarizes the scientific basis for the mental health features in Mindful Organizer.
**Note:** This application is a self-help tool, not a medical device.

## 1. Mood Tracking & Self-Monitoring

**Mechanism**: Regular monitoring of internal states increases emotional self-awareness and regulation.
**Key Evidence**:
- **CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)** uses "mood diaries" to help patients identify triggers and patterns.
- **Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA)** studies show that the act of tracking itself can have a regulatory effect.
- **Bipolar Disorder**: Identifying early warning signs (prodromes) of mania/depression can prevent relapse (Gitlin et al., 2010).

**Implication for App**:
- We use a **1-5 scale** plus symptom tags (Sleep, Irritability) rather than complex text entry to lower friction (Behavioral Activation principle).
- We support **dual-tracking** (positive and negative affect), as positive affect is distinct from lack of negative affect (PANAS scale logic).

## 2. Gamification for ADHD & Motivation

**Mechanism**: External feedback loops (points, streaks) substitute for deficits in internal dopamine reward pathways common in ADHD.
**Key Evidence**:
- **Dopamine Deficit Theory**: ADHD brains struggle to maintain motivation for delayed rewards. Immediate feedback helps.
- **Video Game Therapy**: Research suggests gamified systems can improve executive function and task persistence in ADHD adults (Durell et al., 2018).

**Implication for App**:
- **"ADHD Mode"**: High-frequency visual rewards (badges, "dopamine hits") for small tasks.
- **Visual Timers**: Externalizing time (Pomodoro) combats "time blindness" (Barkley, 1997).

## 3. Digital Organization & Anxiety

**Mechanism**: Environmental clutter correlates with cortisol levels (stress). Reducing external chaos creates a sense of control ("agency").
**Key Evidence**:
- **"Clutter Effect"**: Studies show physical/digital clutter competes for visual attention, reducing processing capacity (McMains & Kastner, 2011).
- **Agency & Anxiety**: Regaining control over one's environment is a core mechanism in reducing anxiety symptoms.

**Implication for App**:
- The "Smart Organize" feature isn't just a utility; it's a **anxiety-reduction tool**.
- The UI uses **"Calm Tech"** principles (amber/blue light safe colors, no flashing) for the Anxiety profile.

## 4. DBT Skills (Dialectical Behavior Therapy)

**Mechanism**: Specific skills (Distress Tolerance, Mindfulness) dampen high emotional arousal.
**Key Evidence**:
- **DBT Efficacy**: Gold-standard treatment for Borderline Personality Disorder and emotional dysregulation (Linehan, 1993).
- **Skills Deficit Model**: Problems arise from a lack of skills to handle intense emotions.

**Implication for App**:
- **"Panic Button"**: Quick access to TIPP skills (Temperature, Intense Exercise, Paced Breathing, Paired Muscle Relaxation).
- **Digital Diary Card**: Tracking skill usage reinforces learning.
