## Title
Mindful Organizer: Calm Your Digital Space

## Tagline
Organize your files, plan your day, and reduce digital stress with evidence-based tools.

## Description
Mindful Organizer is not just a file manager—it's a productivity companion designed for your mental well-being. Whether you have ADHD, anxiety, or just a messy desktop, Mindful Organizer helps you regain control with kindness and clarity.

**Features:**

📂 **Smart File Organization**
- One-click scan to declutter your Downloads, Desktop, or Documents.
- **Safe & Secure**: Always preview changes before they happen. "Undo" anytime.
- **Privacy First**: All processing happens on your device. No cloud uploads.

🧠 **Personalized to Your Brain**
- Choose your profile (e.g., "Focus Challenges", "Anxiety", "Low Mood").
- The app adapts its interface, reminders, and gamification to suit YOU.

📊 **Mood & Progress Tracking**
- Track your mood and energy levels alongside your tasks.
- Log DBT/CBT skills usage to support your therapy goals.

⚡ **Evidence-Based Design**
- Built on principles from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and ADHD coaching research.
- "Calm Mode" reduces visual overstimulation.
- "Gamification" provides dopamine-friendly rewards for small wins.

**Disclaimer**: Mindful Organizer is a self-help tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or therapy.
