Hearth - Privacy Policy

Your privacy and mental well-being are our highest priorities.

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Introduction

Hearth ("the App") is a mental health-aware productivity and task management application developed by Nicholas Toledo ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Our core commitment: Hearth does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. All information you enter into the App remains entirely on your device under your control.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. Hearth operates entirely offline on your local device. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected, stored on remote servers, or transmitted over the internet.
  • No telemetry or usage analytics are gathered.
  • No crash reports are sent automatically.
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking technologies are used.
  • No cookies or web beacons are employed.

Local Data Storage

All data you create within Hearth -- including tasks, notes, schedules, wellness entries, and project files -- is stored exclusively on your local device in the App's data directory.

  • Location: Data is saved in your user profile's application data folder.
  • Format: Data is stored in local database files and .mindful project files.
  • Encryption: Sensitive content, particularly mental health-related entries, is encrypted at rest using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms. Encryption keys are derived from your local device credentials and are never transmitted.

Mental Health Data

We recognize that mental health information is among the most sensitive personal data. We treat it with the highest level of care.

Any wellness tracking, mood logs, or mental health-related content you enter:

  • Is stored only on your local device.
  • Is encrypted at rest to protect against unauthorized access.
  • Is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • Is never used for profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond your personal use within the App.
  • Can be permanently deleted at any time through the App's data management settings.

Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties. Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing to share, sell, rent, or disclose to advertisers, data brokers, or any other entities.

The App does not integrate with third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

Internet Access

The App requests the internetClient capability to support potential future features such as optional cloud synchronization. In the current version (1.0), no network connections are made. Should cloud sync be introduced in a future release, it will be:

  • Entirely optional and opt-in.
  • Clearly disclosed with an updated privacy policy before activation.
  • End-to-end encrypted so that only you can read your data.

Your Control Over Your Data

You have complete control over all data within Hearth:

  • Export: Export all your data at any time in standard formats (JSON, CSV) for backup or migration purposes.
  • Import: Import data from supported formats and previous backups.
  • Delete: Permanently delete any or all of your data from within the App. Deletion is immediate and irreversible -- no data is retained in hidden caches or backups managed by us.
  • Portability: Your .mindful project files are self-contained and can be moved between devices at your discretion.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Hearth does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no data from any user, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

The App is designed for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through some other channel related to this App, please contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. If we make material changes -- particularly if a future version introduces cloud features -- we will provide prominent notice within the App before the changes take effect.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.
