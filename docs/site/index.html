Hearth

A desktop that adapts to your psychology.

The Hearth Project is a desktop-native psychological operating-system layer for people managing ADHD, anxiety, depression, OCD, PTSD, or bipolar disorder. Closes apps when anxiety spikes. Dims the screen when energy is low. Protects focus before you notice you need it. All data stays on your device — no cloud, no telemetry.
