# Shipping Brand Asset Provenance

The shipping files `app-icon.png` and `hero-illustration.png` are generated by
`scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs` using deterministic geometry, gradients,
and seeded texture. They use no stock imagery, model output, copied artwork,
external fonts, or remote resources.

_Branding correction — 2026-08-28: the creator/date lines above preserve the
original generation record. The same deterministic assets were regenerated
byte-for-byte after the visible names changed to Ample on 2026-08-19 and
Paulatim on 2026-08-28; no third-party or model-generated imagery was added._

Earlier PNGs with undocumented provenance are retained unchanged under
`resources/vault/unverified-2026-07-14/`. They are excluded from packaging and
must not be restored to shipping paths without a documented rights review.
