## Evaluation and portfolio

### Bet A — Fast: official Hearth Microsoft Store package

- **Frame:** A1.5.
- **Price hypothesis:** $14.99 one time.
- **Target arithmetic:** Microsoft documents a 15% commerce fee for non-game
  apps. `$14.99 × 0.85 = $12.7415` net per unit; 314 units = **$4,000.83**.
- **Funnel:** Microsoft Store search/category/recommendation → product page →
  Store checkout. There is no existing owned-audience assumption.
- **Falsifier:** Kill or materially reposition after two certification failures,
  or after 100 observed Store product-page views with zero purchases. The clock
  starts only when the listing is actually live.
- **Current gate:** Modern cryptography for stored personal information, asset
  provenance, support channel, fresh Windows build, screenshots, and manual
  paid submission.

### Bet B — Heavy: Hearth-to-Production Electron Kit on itch.io

- **Frame:** A4.6, offered only after it contains new clean demo code, threat
  model, migration guide, tests, and rights-clean documentation beyond Hearth's
  existing MIT files.
- **Price hypothesis:** $249 one time.
- **Target arithmetic:** At itch.io's default 10% revenue share plus its typical
  2.9% + $0.30 processor fee, estimated per-sale net is **$216.58**. Nineteen
  sales = $4,115.00; subtract itch.io's one-time $3 tax-identity adjustment =
  **$4,112.00**. Payout transfer fees are not yet known and must be deducted.
- **Funnel:** itch.io Assets search/browse → product page → itch.io checkout.
- **Falsifier:** Drop if the owner cannot complete a zero-upfront seller setup,
  if marketplace policy rejects the asset category, or after 100 page views with
  fewer than two purchases.
- **Current gate:** New exclusive deliverable does not yet exist; seller/tax and
  payout method are not connected. itch.io states revenue waits seven days, then
  payout review usually takes another 10–14 days, so Day-28 cash timing is risky.

No third bet is active. A standalone Focus Guard Store app is the first reserve,
but adding it before either active bet reaches a falsifier would dilute work.
